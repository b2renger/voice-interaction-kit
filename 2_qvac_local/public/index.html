Turn-taking

Short = an impatient agent that cuts in at pauses. Long = a patient agent, but slower to reply.

With Smart Turn, a complete sentence ends the turn quickly; a pause mid-sentence waits up to the end-of-turn silence. Try 2000 ms.

Above 0, the agent first lowers its voice. A short "mm-hm" lets it carry on; a short "stop" makes it stop and listen.

With speakers, untick "Listens while speaking": otherwise it hears itself. With headphones, leave it ticked.
